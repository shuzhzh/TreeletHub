1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Text you enter for translation (for example, typed input).
  • Voice input if you use voice translation features.
  • Camera-captured content if you use camera/OCR translation features.
  • Translation results and history stored locally on your device if history is enabled.
  • Technical data such as app version, device type, and diagnostics needed to maintain app stability.

We design Treelate to prioritize on-device processing whenever possible.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only for legitimate app functions, including:

  • Providing translation services (text, voice, camera/OCR).
  • Generating and displaying translation output.
  • Saving optional translation history on your device.
  • Improving feature quality, reliability, and performance.
  • Protecting the App against abuse, errors, and security risks.

3. Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Microphone: required for voice translation input.
  • Camera: required for camera-based text recognition and translation.

You can revoke permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Translation history is stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated.
  • You can clear translation history inside the App at any time.
  • We retain data only as long as necessary for the features you use and legal compliance.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

If certain translation functions rely on platform or third-party services, only the minimum required data may be processed to fulfill your request, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to data associated with your use of the App.
  • Request correction or deletion of data.
  • Withdraw consent for certain processing where consent is the legal basis.

To exercise rights, contact us via the email listed below.

9. International Users

If you use the App from outside the country where services are operated, your information may be processed in jurisdictions with different data protection laws.
