Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

TreeletAge (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the TreeletAge app and the website treelet.us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We are committed to privacy-by-design. Most processing happens on your device.

  • Health & questionnaire data — Data you provide or authorize from Apple Health (e.g. heart rate, sleep, activity) and answers to in-app questionnaires are used only on your device to compute your biological age and related insights. We do not upload or store this data on our servers unless you explicitly use a feature that requires it (e.g. syncing or backup, if offered).
  • Photos — Selfie or photo inputs are processed locally for appearance-related signals. We do not retain or upload your photos to our servers.
  • Account & subscription — If you create an account or subscribe, we may collect email, account identifier, and subscription status, in line with Apple’s App Store and our payment processor’s policies.
  • Usage & diagnostics — We may collect anonymized usage and crash data to improve the app (e.g. via Apple’s analytics). We do not use this for advertising or cross-site tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the TreeletAge app and treelet.us.
  • Compute and display your biological age and related reports on your device.
  • Manage your account and subscription, and send essential service communications.
  • Comply with law and protect our rights and users’ safety.

3. Data Storage and Security

Health and questionnaire data used for your age estimate are processed and stored on your device. We use industry-standard practices to protect any data we do store (e.g. account and subscription data) and do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, and payments (e.g. Apple). Their use of data is governed by their respective privacy policies. We do not share your health or photo data with third parties for advertising.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. For data held on your device, you can delete the app and associated data. For data we hold, contact us using the details below.

6. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13 (or higher age where required). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on treelet.us and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at: privacy@treelet.us (or the contact address published on treelet.us).
