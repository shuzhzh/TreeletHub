Core Features

Voice journaling, health context, and on-device AI

🧭 Mood Panorama Timeline

See daily mood and health snapshots in one place to spot connections between emotional and physical states.

🎙️ Voice Diary

Record by voice and transcribe into text for faster, lower-friction daily journaling.

🩺 Health Context

Optionally combine sleep, workouts, and mindfulness data so entries reflect your real day with less manual work.

🤝 On-Device AI Mirror Feedback

On supported devices, local models can provide empathetic summaries and perspective prompts with no forced cloud processing.

📈 Weekly Review & Growth

Weekly summaries and simple comparisons help you review patterns and changes over time.

🔒 Privacy First

App lock and list blur are supported; health and diary data are primarily stored on device.
