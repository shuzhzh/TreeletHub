Core Features

AI cases · Career progression · Real workflow

🩺 AI Case Generation

DeepSeek AI generates unlimited realistic cases; each patient has unique symptoms, history, and signs; difficulty scales with your level.

📈 Career Progression

Start as an intern and advance by accurate diagnosis: Resident (cure 100) → Attending (300) → Associate Chief (600) → Chief (1000). Each step brings higher reputation and harder cases.

🔄 Real Diagnosis Workflow

Consultation, physical exam, lab and imaging, specialist referral when needed, referral when low on stamina—then diagnose and treat to cure patients.

⚖️ Resource Management

Balance stamina, mental health, reputation, and money. Correct diagnosis rewards exp and reputation; mistakes can cost your license. Manage wisely to become a top doctor.

About Sim Doctor

Sim Doctor is a medical career simulation game for medical enthusiasts, students, and strategy fans—learn diagnosis workflow and clinical thinking through play.

Important Notice
This app is a simulation game. All cases and diagnoses are AI-generated for entertainment and learning only; they do not replace real medical care. Please consult a qualified doctor for health concerns.
