Launch desktop apps from your phone—without leaving the couch.

TreeletHub is a computer companion that turns your favorite apps into a clean, customizable grid—and lets your phone act as a remote launcher on the same Wi‑Fi network.

Build your hub on the computer
• Arrange apps in a 3×3 grid—drag to reorder or move tiles between slots
• Add system shortcuts: volume, brightness, media controls, screenshots, copy/paste, and custom URLs
• Create multiple pages for more apps (subscription)
• Pair phones with a simple six-digit code—no account required

Control from your phone
• Install the free TreeletHub companion app on your phone
• Connect on the same Wi‑Fi and tap any tile to launch apps on the computer
• Layout stays in sync between computer and phone
• Perfect when the computer is at the desk and you’re across the room

Floating island mode (subscription)
Bring a floating strip to the top of your desktop:
• Now playing media info, plus media shortcuts
• Clock with optional hourly chime
• Local weather and forecasts
• Clipboard history, file staging, and nearby hand-off
• Hub grid and shortcuts—same as on your phone
• Collapse to a slim capsule when you want a quieter desktop

Designed for your network
TreeletHub works over your local Wi‑Fi. Pairing and sync stay on your LAN—your hub, your home network.

Languages: English and Simplified Chinese.

Requirements
• A computer running TreeletHub
• TreeletHub for phone (free) for remote launch and pairing
• Phone and computer on the same Wi‑Fi; grant Local Network permission on the phone when prompted

Subscription
Subscribe to unlock multiple hub pages and floating island mode. Manage or cancel anytime in System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Subscriptions auto-renew until cancelled.
