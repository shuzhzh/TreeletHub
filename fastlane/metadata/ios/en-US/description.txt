TreeletHub is your phone productivity launcher: a honeycomb wall for the apps you open every day—and a Wi‑Fi remote for the apps and shortcuts on your computer companion.

On your phone alone
• Honeycomb launcher: search and add launchable apps with clear artwork
• Home Screen widgets: hand off from the widget straight into the target app
• One-tap calling: pick contacts, then dial from the honeycomb
• System camera: jump to the system camera capture flow when you need a photo
• Open apps that provide a public launch link; only launchable apps can be added

When paired with the computer companion
• Tap to remote-launch desktop apps, web shortcuts, and system actions
• Use Keyboard & Mouse remote when your computer companion supports it
• Two-finger swipe down to show the desktop; inline volume, brightness, and media controls
• Layouts sync with the companion; multiple phones can connect at once

Privacy & connection
• Short pairing code; discovery and sync stay on your local network by default
• Core pairing and remote control need no cloud account

Set up your phone honeycomb anytime. To remote-control a computer, install the companion and pair on the same network.
