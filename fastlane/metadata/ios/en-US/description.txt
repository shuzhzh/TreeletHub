TreeletHub is a productivity companion for your home or office network.

On your computer, arrange favorite apps, links, and shortcuts in a synced grid. On your phone—on the same trusted Wi‑Fi—tap to launch or switch without walking back to the desk.

Features:
• Pair with a short code; no cloud account required for core sync
• Drag-and-drop grid on both sides
• Optional extra hub pages with subscription
• Local network only for discovery and control

Install the desktop companion from the Mac App Store to use remote launch. This iPhone app is the remote control for that hub.

Privacy: pairing and layout sync stay on your local network unless you use other Treelet services separately.
