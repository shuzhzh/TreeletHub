TreeletHub is your Wi‑Fi remote for the apps and shortcuts on your computer.

Arrange a honeycomb launcher on the desktop companion, then tap from your phone on the same trusted network—no cloud account needed for core pairing and sync.

What’s new in this release:
• Honeycomb phone launcher with App Store icons and one-tap open for apps that publish a public URL
• Home Screen widget that hands off into TreeletHub, then opens the target app
• One-tap calling: pick contacts, then dial from the honeycomb
• System camera capture when the Camera app has no public launch URL
• Search the App Store catalog; only launchable apps can be added

Also included:
• Short pairing code on your local network
• Synced layout with the desktop companion
• Local-network discovery and control by default

Install the desktop companion first, then use this iPhone app as the remote. Pairing and layout sync stay on your LAN unless you use other Treelet services separately.
